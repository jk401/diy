# Controlled DIY Cannabis Drying Cabinet with Capillary Humidifier`n`n## Project Goal`nCreate a low-cost, silent drying cabinet maintaining a constant humidity of 60% RH for 14 days (slow & low curing method), focusing on mold prevention and reliable isolation.`n`n---`n`n## Key Specifications (Current Setup)`n`n### 1. Cabinet Construction`n- **Material & Dimensions:** 7-layer corrugated cardboard box (dimensions: 120 x 80 x 80 cm, extremely robust, heavy-duty structure, no internal plastic foil needed, utilizes natural humidity-buffering properties of cardboard).`n- **Structural Stability:** Due to the heavy 7-layer build, no additional internal wooden frames are required to support the hanging weight.`n`n### 2. Capillary Humidifier`n- **Reservoir:** 45l IKEA Samla box (57 x 39 x 28 cm; source: https://www.ikea.com/sg/en/p/samla-box-with-lid-transparent-s39440767/), filled with approx. 20 liters of water for a fully maintenance-free 14-day run.`n- **Principle & Evaporation Media:** Evaporation via capillary wicks using hydroponic non-woven spreader mat (NFT Spreader Mat 1m x 40cm; source: https://www.higarden.cz/prislusenstvi-pro-nft/netkana-textilie-spreader-mat-pro-techniku-nft-1m-x-40cm/).`n- **Internal Construction:** Inside the box, the spreader mat is wrapped in a double layer around a spiral-shaped wire mesh/netting, which holds the fabric rigidly together and maximizes the vertical evaporation surface area.`n- **Airflow & Lid Configuration:** The lid features two Arctic P14 PWM PST fans blowing air into the box. The box has ventilation holes along the sides, and internal plastic deflectors ensure humid air is smoothly guided out without blowing directly onto the hanging flowers.`n`n### 3. Ventilation`n- **Fans:** **Arctic P14 PWM PST** (140 mm pressure-optimized fans with PWM Sharing Technology; source: https://www.arctic.de/en/P14-PWM-PST/ACFAN00125A/).`n- **Layout & Configuration:**`n  - **Humidifier (Samla box):** Equipped with 2 x Arctic P14 fans to efficiently push air across the evaporation wicks.`n  - **Main Exhaust:** Equipped with 1 x Arctic P14 fan connected via a 3D-printed reduction (140 mm to 125 mm) into a 125 mm alu-flex exhaust duct.`n  - **Passive Intake:** Generous 200 mm alu-flex duct ensuring very low resistance and smooth fresh air intake.`n- **Power & Regulation:** Operated on boosted voltage (approx. 6 to 8 V stepped up from 5V USB using an AliExpress DC-DC step-up converter with a rotary potentiometer knob for precise fan speed adjustment) for whisper-quiet operation and zero airflow draft.`n`n### 4. Climate Control`n- **Hygrostat:** Digital hygrostat **XH-W3005** (12V version, 0-99% RH), dedicated exclusively to controlling the humidifier (Samla box fans).`n- **Control Logic & Operation:**`n  - **Days 1 to 4 (High Evaporation Phase):** Fresh harvest releases massive moisture. The exhaust fan is manually adjusted to a higher speed setting so it continuously removes excess humidity while occasionally balancing out with the humidifier.`n  - **Days 5 to 14 (Stabilization Phase):** After the initial drying period, the exhaust fan is turned down to its silent minimum speed, and the XH-W3005 hygrostat takes over completely, running exclusively on the humidifier to maintain the target 60% RH.`n- **Power Supply:** IKEA SMAHAGEL 3-port USB charger (providing 5V DC power, stepped up via DC-DC converter, placed externally outside the drying cabinet).`n`n---`n`n## Estimated Cost Breakdown (Budget)`n`n| Item | Description / Model | Est.
